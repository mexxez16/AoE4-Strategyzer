        The Essence of Strategy

        "Strategy is the necessary response to the inescapable reality of limited resources. No entity, regardless of size, has unlimited resources. Strategy, therefore, is about making choices on how we will concentrate our limited resources to achieve competitive advantage. All else follows from there."
        — Von Clausewitz according to Pieterson
        "Strategy is choice" & "Set of choices about winning."
        — Roger Martin
        "Everyone has a plan 'till they get punched in the mouth."
        — Mike Tyson
        "A strategy is a way through a difficulty, an approach to overcoming an obstacle, a response to a challenge. If the challenge is not defined, it is difficult or impossible to assess the quality of the strategy. And, if you cannot assess that, you cannot reject a bad strategy or improve a good one."
        — Richard Rumelt
        "The supreme art of war is to subdue the enemy without fighting."
        — Sun Tzu
        "All warfare is based on deception. Hence, when we are able to attack, we must seem unable; when using our forces, we must appear inactive; when we are near, we must make the enemy believe we are far away; when far away, we must make him believe we are near."
        — Sun Tzu
        "If you know the enemy and know yourself, you need not fear the result of a hundred battles."
        — Sun Tzu
        "Strategy is the use of the engagement for the purpose of the war."
        — Carl von Clausewitz
        "Tactics teaches the use of armed forces in the engagement; strategy, the use of engagements for the object of the war."
        — Carl von Clausewitz
        "War is nothing but a continuation of politics with the admixture of other means."
        — Carl von Clausewitz
        "The talent of the strategist is to identify the decisive point and to concentrate everything on it, removing forces from secondary fronts and ignoring lesser objectives."
        — Carl von Clausewitz
        "Strategy is the art of making war upon the map, and comprehends the whole theater of operations."
        — Antoine-Henri Jomini
        "The fundamental principle of war is to concentrate the mass of your forces against fractions of the enemy."
        — Antoine-Henri Jomini
        "Strategy is a system of expedients; it is more than a mere scholarly discipline."
        — Helmuth von Moltke the Elder
        "Strategy is a system of makeshifts. It is more than a science. It is bringing knowledge to bear on practical life, the further elaboration of an original guiding idea under constantly changing circumstances. It is the art of acting under the pressure of the most demanding conditions."
        — Helmuth von Moltke the Elder
        "No plan of operations reaches with any certainty beyond the first encounter with the enemy's main force."
        — Helmuth von Moltke the Elder
        "Throughout the ages, effective results in war have rarely been attained unless the approach has had such indirectness as to ensure the opponent's unreadiness to meet it… In strategy, the longest way round is often the shortest way home."
        — B.H. Liddell Hart
        "The art of distributing and applying military means to fulfill the ends of policy."
        — B.H. Liddell Hart
        "Strategy has for its purpose the reduction of fighting to the slenderest possible proportions."
        — B.H. Liddell Hart
        "What is strategy? A mental tapestry of changing intentions for harmonizing and focusing our efforts as a basis for realizing some aim or purpose in an unfolding and often unforeseen world of many bewildering events and many contending interests."
        — John Boyd
        "Strategy is the art of the dialectic of force or, more precisely, the art of the dialectic of two opposing wills using force to resolve their dispute."
        — André Beaufre
